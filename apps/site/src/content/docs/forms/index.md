**Status:** planned, number 5 on the [roadmap](/roadmap/). Nothing here is built yet.

Forms is forms and surveys.

## Planned scope

- Question types: short and long text, choice, scale, date, file upload
- Sections, branching logic and validation
- Responses stored in Databases and viewable as Sheets
- Export of the form and responses

## How it will be offered

Like [Documents](/documents/), Forms will come in two layers:

- **Ready-made UI:** a form builder, a response-collecting view, and a results summary.
- **Build your own:** a headless engine on the same operations, selection and history foundation as Documents, so you can drive it from your own interface, from Swift or Kotlin code, or from a script.

It will run on the same portable engine as every other editor ([Architecture](/platform/architecture/)).
