**Status:** planned, number 4 on the [roadmap](/roadmap/). Nothing here is built yet.

Boards is a free-form canvas like Freeform or Miro.

## Planned scope

- An infinite canvas with notes, shapes, connectors, images and freehand drawing
- Frames, grouping and alignment tools
- Export to image and PDF

## How it will be offered

Like [Documents](/documents/), Boards will come in two layers:

- **Ready-made UI:** a pan-and-zoom canvas with a tool palette and object inspector.
- **Build your own:** a headless engine on the same operations, selection and history foundation as Documents, so you can drive it from your own interface, from Swift or Kotlin code, or from a script.

It will run on the same portable engine as every other editor ([Architecture](/platform/architecture/)).
