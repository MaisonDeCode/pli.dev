**Status:** planned, number 7 on the [roadmap](/roadmap/). Nothing here is built yet.

Databases is structured records like Airtable or Notion databases.

## Planned scope

- Typed fields, records and relations
- Views: table, board, calendar and gallery
- Filters, sorting, grouping and formulas
- A shared store that Forms, Sheets and Documents can read and write

## How it will be offered

Like [Documents](/documents/), Databases will come in two layers:

- **Ready-made UI:** table and board views with a field editor and a filter bar.
- **Build your own:** a headless engine on the same operations, selection and history foundation as Documents, so you can drive it from your own interface, from Swift or Kotlin code, or from a script.

It will run on the same portable engine as every other editor ([Architecture](/platform/architecture/)).
