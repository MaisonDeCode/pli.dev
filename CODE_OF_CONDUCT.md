# Code of Conduct

Pli is made by people who are glad to have help. To keep it a place where that is true, everyone who takes part in the project (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space that belongs to it) agrees to the following.

- **Be respectful.** Treat people the way you would want to be treated. Disagree with ideas and code, not with the person who wrote them.
- **Be welcoming.** People come with different experience, languages and backgrounds. Answer questions kindly, and do not make anyone feel stupid for asking.
- **Keep it on topic and keep it clean.** No harassment, insults, threats, slurs, sexual attention that is not wanted, or publishing someone's private information.
- **Assume good faith, and say when you are not sure.** Ask what someone meant before you decide it was meant badly.

## What happens when it goes wrong

The maintainers can edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who break this code, to protect the people taking part. If someone is harassing you, or you see it, write to the maintainers through the contact on [maisondecode.com](https://maisondecode.com) (or, on GitHub, report the comment). Reports are kept private.

This code applies in the spaces of the project, and when someone speaks for the project in public.
