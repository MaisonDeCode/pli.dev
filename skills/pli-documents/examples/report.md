# Quarterly Report

[TOC]

## Summary

Revenue grew **12%** over the last quarter, driven by the new *Pro* plan. Churn fell to 2.1%, the lowest in two years. Read the [full dashboard](https://example.com/dashboard) for the detail.

> The best quarter we have had since the product launched.

## Results

| Region | Revenue | Change |
|:-------|--------:|-------:|
| North  | $1.2M   | +14%   |
| South  | $0.9M   | +9%    |
| West   | $1.4M   | +15%   |

### What drove it

- The **Pro** plan launched in March
  - 40% of new customers chose it
  - Average order value rose 18%
- Support response time halved
- A new onboarding email series

### Next steps

1. Hire two support engineers
2. Ship the reporting module
3. Review pricing in the fourth quarter
